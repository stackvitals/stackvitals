# StackVitals and Swarmia

Swarmia: A commercial engineering-effectiveness product: DORA, developer surveys, AI-adoption tracking and software capitalization.

**Kind:** SaaS; free up to 9 developers. **How it runs:** Hosted on Google Cloud in Frankfurt by default; GitHub App. $45-55 a developer a month above 9.

Checked 2026-11-01 from the sources below; tools change, so check them before you rely on this.

## Where Swarmia is better

- Developer experience surveys with benchmarks; working agreements with Slack nudges.
- Investment and capitalization reports; AI-tool adoption across vendors; polish and support.

## Where StackVitals differs

- Free at any size, and your data stays in your repo.
- Test-level flaky triage; Swarmia's CI view shows job failure rates, not flaky tests.
- No surveys: StackVitals reads history only.
